Privacy Policy & Local Execution Guarantee

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 · 100% Offline & Privacy-First Architecture

Overview

This application is designed with absolute privacy in mind. It is a 100% locally hosted, offline application. We believe your hardware asset and maintenance data belongs solely to you, which is why the application operates entirely on your device without transmitting data anywhere else.

1. Zero Telemetry & No Tracking

We do not collect, track, or aggregate any analytics, telemetry, usage statistics, crash reports, or diagnostic data. Zero telemetry and zero trackers exist.

2. No User Accounts Required

No sign-up or authentication is necessary. Preferences and custom themes are saved locally and are never linked to any external cloud identity.

3. Device-Bound Storage

All data generated, processed, or saved while using this application remains strictly on your local machine. The application does not communicate with external servers.

4. Offline Operation

The application makes zero outbound internet requests during normal operation, guaranteeing complete operational sovereignty.
