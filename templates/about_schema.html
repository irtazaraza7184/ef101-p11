Database Tables & Constraints

locations

Physical deployment zones and IP subnets for hardware assets.

ColumnTypeConstraint
nameTEXTPK Lab 1, Lab 2...
prefixTEXTUNIQUE, Asset ID prefix (L1)
ip_subnetINTEGERUNIQUE, CHECK (1..254)

assets

Computer hardware fleet catalogue, specs, network IPs, and open fault state.

ColumnTypeConstraint
asset_idTEXTPK e.g. L1-PC-001
locationTEXTFK locations(name)
ip_addressTEXTUNIQUE(location, ip_address)
statusTEXTOperational | Faulty | Under Repair

maintenance

Permanent repair log and completed fault events.

ColumnTypeConstraint
idINTEGERPK AUTOINCREMENT
asset_idTEXTFK assets(asset_id)
cost_usdREALCHECK (cost_usd >= 0)

fault_steps

Intermediate diagnostic and work steps while an asset fault remains open.

ColumnTypeConstraint
idINTEGERPK AUTOINCREMENT
asset_idTEXTFK assets(asset_id)
step_dateTEXTISO date YYYY-MM-DD
